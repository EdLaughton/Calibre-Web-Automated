      Your requests

        How requests work
        1. Choose a Hardcover work. Existing books open directly in your library.
        2. The worker checks identity, exclusions, English EPUB editions, duplicates and pending downloads. Eligible releases are sent to Bookshelf.
        3. Bookshelf downloads through its configured client. The guarded handoff verifies the completed file and gives it to CWA for normal import.
        4. Needs review shows the reason below your request. An administrator investigates the exact Bookshelf grab and handoff record, fixes the cause, then approves continued guarded processing or denies the request with a reason.

        A book added directly in Bookshelf follows the same guarded handoff. It is shown here only if someone also requested that Hardcover work.
